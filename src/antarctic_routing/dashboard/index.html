Plan an Antarctic passage

Choose where the ship sails from, where it is going and the day it leaves. The planner compares routes across sea ice, drifting icebergs, wind and current, and holds the modelled risk to a 5% budget.

Unavailable Route planning is not available on this server

Plan Route runs the real historical model (Real Historical Data). Nothing synthetic is shown in its place.

Routing area

Choose a start and destination from the lists, or press Pick on map and click open water.

What goes into a plan

  1. StartA named place, or any point of open water you pick on the map.
  2. DestinationA point on land or in ice-locked water moves to the nearest open-water routing cell, and the page shows how far it moved.
  3. Departure dateA past date replays Real Historical Data. A future date in the supported range gives a labelled Forecast / hackathon estimate.
  4. Plan Route200 joint sea-ice and iceberg scenarios pick the departure and the route that keep the risk within the 5% budget.

Every result is a research estimate with its assumptions in view. Check the daily simulation and the sources behind it before reading anything into a number.
