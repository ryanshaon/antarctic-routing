Before you depart

From a route to a
voyage decision.

  1. StartWhere the ship sails from: a named location or a point picked on the map.
  2. DestinationWhere it is going. A point on land or in ice-locked water moves to the nearest open-water routing cell, and the page shows how far.
  3. Departure dateA past date replays Real Historical Data; a future date in the supported range gives a labelled Forecast / hackathon estimate.
  4. Plan Route200 joint sea-ice and iceberg scenarios pick the departure and route that keep the risk within the 5% budget.
